Payvand Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Respecting your privacy

Payvand respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we use it, and what choices you have.

1

Information we collect

To provide the service, we may store:

  • Original and shortened URLs and creation time
  • User or guest identifiers
  • Account phone number, username, or display name
  • Hashed passwords and temporary one-time codes
  • Created QR codes, contact messages, and abuse reports
  • Technical click data
2

Click data and analytics

When someone opens a short link, we may record the time, IP address, user agent, browser, operating system, device type, country, city, and referrer. This supports analytics, security, abuse detection, and service improvement.

3

Cookies

Cookies support guest sessions, link limits, and signed-in sessions. You can manage them in your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect parts of the site.

4

Accounts and authentication

Signing in and account management may use a phone number, password, or one-time code. Passwords are stored as hashes; one-time codes are used for sign in or password recovery.

5

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage short links
  • Show analytics and create QR codes
  • Authenticate users and limit abuse
  • Check unsafe links and review abuse reports
  • Respond to messages and improve the service
6

Link safety checks

To reduce harmful links, URLs may be checked with security services such as Google Safe Browsing. The URL is sent to that service for a safety check.

7

Information sharing

Payvand does not sell user data or share it for advertising. Data may be processed or disclosed when needed to deliver the service, check safety, maintain systems, or meet legal requirements.

8

Retention and deletion

Links, click data, and QR codes are kept as long as needed for the service, security, reports, or technical requirements. Signed-in users can delete their links and QR codes from the dashboard. Contact support to request account data review or deletion.

9

Data security

We use authentication, password hashing, access limits, and suspicious link checks to protect data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10

Contact information

For privacy questions, deletion requests, or problem reports, contact us through the website form or email payvandweb@gmail.com to get in touch.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version and its update date will appear on this page.
